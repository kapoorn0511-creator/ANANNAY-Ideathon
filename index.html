About PlacementShield

🎯 The Problem We Solve

Every year, thousands of engineering students get rejected in placement rounds not because they lack skills — but because they cannot defend what they built. Interviewers probe resume projects aggressively, and students who copied from tutorials freeze under pressure.

No tool specifically trains students to answer their own resume's questions under time pressure. PlacementShield is built exactly for this gap.

📊 Evidence We Collected

We didn't just assume this was a problem. We interviewed 5 students in detail and surveyed 62 across Tier 1, 2, and 3 colleges to validate this pain point.

Full interview transcripts and survey data are available in our evidence/ folder in the repository.

📂 View Evidence Data →

🛠️ Tech Stack

  • ⚡ Vanilla HTML5 / CSS3 / JavaScript — Zero dependencies, instant load
  • 🤖 Client-side AI simulation engine (expandable to Gemini API)
  • 🎨 Custom design system with dark mode
  • 📱 Fully responsive — works on mobile and laptop
