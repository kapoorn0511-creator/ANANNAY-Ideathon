# Student Interview Notes

As part of our research for the ANANNAY Ideathon (Problem Statement 4), we interviewed 5 students across different years and tiers of engineering colleges to understand their pain points regarding placement preparation and mental load.

### Interview 1: The "LeetCode Grinder"
- **Name/Profile:** Aryan T. (3rd Year, Tier-2 Engineering College)
- **Pain Point:** Spent 6 months solving 400+ algorithmic puzzles, but got rejected in a top-tier interview because he lacked system design and practical API knowledge.
- **Mental Load:** High frustration and burnout. Feels the "standard prep path" is a scam.
- **Direct Quote:** *"I spent 6 months reversing linked lists, but in the interview, they asked me to design an API gateway. I felt completely betrayed by the standard prep path. It's so demoralizing solving puzzles that have no real-world application."*

### Interview 2: The Isolated & Stressed Coder
- **Name/Profile:** Sneha M. (4th Year, Tier-3 College)
- **Pain Point:** Lack of a peer group and immense isolation during the placement season. 
- **Mental Load:** High anxiety and depression symptoms. 
- **Direct Quote:** *"Preparing for placements is incredibly lonely. When I get stuck on a system design concept or keep getting rejected, there's no one to talk to. The stress is worse than the actual coding. I just want a safe space to vent with people who understand."*

### Interview 3: The Misguided Learner
- **Name/Profile:** Rohan K. (Recent Graduate)
- **Pain Point:** Followed the university curriculum strictly (scored 9.2 CGPA) but cannot clear technical rounds because he lacks modern tech stack skills.
- **Mental Load:** Regret and loss of confidence.
- **Direct Quote:** *"My college taught us C++ and theoretical networking, but every company wants React, Node, or Cloud experience. The gap is massive and I feel like I wasted 4 years of my life."*

### Interview 4: The Overwhelmed Beginner
- **Name/Profile:** Priya D. (2nd Year, State University)
- **Pain Point:** Paralysis by analysis. The internet has too many roadmaps and conflicting advice.
- **Mental Load:** Extreme overwhelm, leading to procrastination.
- **Direct Quote:** *"I don't know what MNCs actually want. I wish companies just told us directly what skills to learn and gave us their actual problems instead of me guessing from random YouTube roadmaps."*

### Interview 5: The "Lost in the Crowd" Candidate
- **Name/Profile:** Kartik S. (3rd Year, Tier-1 College)
- **Pain Point:** Applies to hundreds of portals but never hears back because traditional resumes don't show his actual problem-solving capability.
- **Mental Load:** Rejection fatigue.
- **Direct Quote:** *"I have the skills, but my resume just gets filtered out by ATS software. If I could just prove my worth by solving a company's actual problem and get noticed directly by HR, it would save me months of anxiety."*

---
**Key Takeaways:**
1. **The Gap is Real:** University curriculums do not match MNC needs.
2. **Burnout is High:** Grinding irrelevant theoretical puzzles destroys motivation.
3. **Isolation is Dangerous:** Students urgently need a peer community to manage the mental load of rejections and prep stress.
