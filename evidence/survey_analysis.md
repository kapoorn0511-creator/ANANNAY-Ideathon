# Survey Analysis — PlacementShield Evidence

**Survey Conducted:** October 2025  
**Sample Size:** 62 Engineering Students  
**Distribution:** Tier-1 (IIT/NIT) = 14, Tier-2 NIT/Private = 24, Tier-3/State = 24  
**Year:** Final Year = 52, Pre-Final = 10  

---

## 📊 Key Statistics

### Q1: "Have you ever frozen or blanked when an interviewer asked questions about your OWN resume projects?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes** | **48** | **77.4%** |
| No | 14 | 22.6% |

> 🔥 **77.4% of students have frozen on their own resume projects in an interview.**  
> *(This validates the core pain point of PlacementShield.)*

---

### Q2: "How many hours do you typically spend preparing anxiously the night before a placement interview?"

| Hours | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| < 1 hour | 8 | 12.9% |
| 1–2 hours | 12 | 19.4% |
| **3–4 hours** | **24** | **38.7%** |
| **5+ hours** | **18** | **29.0%** |

> 📌 **67.7% of students spend 3+ hours in anxious, unfocused preparation** because they don't know what specifically to revise from their resume.

---

### Q3: "Do you use ChatGPT / Gemini for interview preparation?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Yes | 46 | 74.2% |
| No | 16 | 25.8% |

---

### Q4 (ChatGPT users only): "Is ChatGPT helpful for practicing resume defense specifically?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| **No — it's too agreeable, doesn't push back** | **31** | **67.4%** |
| Somewhat helpful | 15 | 32.6% |
| Very helpful | 0 | 0% |

> 📌 **0% of students found ChatGPT "very helpful" for resume defense practice.**  
> **67.4% said it is too polite and doesn't simulate real interviewer pressure.**

---

### Q5: "Has any existing tool specifically helped you prepare to DEFEND your resume projects?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| **No — nothing works for this** | **57** | **91.9%** |
| Yes | 5 | 8.1% |

> 🔥 **91.9% of students found no existing tool helpful for resume defense preparation.**

---

### Q6: "What type of tool would you most want?" (Top responses)

| Tool Type | Count |
|---|---|
| AI that reads MY resume and asks targeted questions | 34 |
| Mock interview with time pressure simulation | 18 |
| Timed cross-examination with scoring and feedback | 7 |
| Other | 3 |

---

### Q7: "Would you pay for this tool if it worked well?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| Yes | 26 | 41.9% |
| No | 36 | 58.1% |

> *(41.9% willingness to pay for a student-facing tool is significantly high. Interviewee Kartik B. specifically said "I'd pay ₹200-300/month and know 10 friends who'd sign up.")*

---

### Q8: "Would you use this tool if it were free?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes** | **62** | **100%** |
| No | 0 | 0% |

> ✅ **100% of surveyed students said they would use PlacementShield if it were free.**

---

### Q9: "What is your BIGGEST fear going into a placement interview?" (Top answers)

1. "Interviewer asks a question about my own project that I can't answer" — **31 responses**
2. "Blanking out / freezing under pressure" — **18 responses**
3. "Being rejected after clearing DSA (in project/technical discussion round)" — **9 responses**
4. Other — 4 responses

---

### Q10: "Would you recommend this tool to your placement batch?"

| Response | Count | Percentage |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes** | **62** | **100%** |
| No | 0 | 0% |

---

## 🎯 Key Insights for Judges

1. **The problem is real and validated**: 77.4% freeze — not a hypothetical.
2. **Existing tools fail**: 91.9% say nothing helps specifically for resume defense.
3. **ChatGPT is not a substitute**: 67.4% say it's "too polite" — lacks pressure simulation.
4. **Demand is clear**: 100% would use it free, 41.9% would even pay.
5. **Students themselves described PlacementShield** before we showed it to them — "Something that reads my resume and grills me with a timer" appeared organically in 8+ interviews.
